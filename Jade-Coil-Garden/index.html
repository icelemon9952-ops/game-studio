Rules

How It Works

  • Normal food grows the snake by 1 and bonus food grows it by 2.
  • Every few pickups add a new spike warning, then that cell becomes solid.
  • Crossing a wall wraps you to the opposite side. Boost lets you pass through your own body for a short time, but not spike cells.
  • Food beside spikes becomes Bold Food and pays extra if you commit to the risky route.
  • Every 4 quick pickups without using Boost triggers a Lotus bonus, so clean chains score higher.
